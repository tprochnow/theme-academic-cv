During her time in the lab, Veronika primarily worked on the SPACES and MTC projects and served as project coordinator for SPACES. She developed a strong interest in ecological momentary assessment, physiological data, and quantitative methods, all of which she was introduced to through her work in the COHESION Lab. A main focus of her research is how childhood trauma negatively impacts physical health, and she is also interested in factors that may mitigate this relationship, such as emotion regulation, resilience, mindfulness, and physical activity. Veronika intends to pursue a faculty career in academia, combining her passion for teaching, mentoring, and research.
