Dr. Jeong-Hui Park is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Health and Exercise Science at the University of Oklahoma. She earned her PhD in Health Education from Texas A&M University in 2025 and subsequently completed postdoctoral research training at The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center. During her doctoral training, she worked with Dr. Prochnow on several research projects examining social networks, physical activity, and chronic disease self-management, including studies involving youth physical activity and type 2 diabetes management among Black/African American men. Her current research focuses on leveraging wearable technologies and intensive longitudinal data to understand physical activity and sedentary behavior and advance chronic disease prevention and healthy aging, particularly among cancer survivors and underserved populations.
