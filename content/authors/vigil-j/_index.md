Jacqueline's participation within the lab has included contributing to systematic literature reviews on social and interpersonal influences on physical activity and co-leading a review of physical activity interventions for rural older adults using the social ecological model. She also gained qualitative research experience through an Engagement by Design project, exploring intergenerational communities and healthy aging in place, and supported Spanish-language interviews for the SPACES study. Through her research, she hopes to advance community-engaged and evidence-based approaches that supports equitable, sustainable changes to improving health and enhance the capacity of institutions serving rural communities.
