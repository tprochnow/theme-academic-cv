Since getting her PhD in Health Education at Texas A&M University, Laurel has worked across academic, nonprofit, and government sectors to promote approaches to healthy and active aging. Regardless of the setting, she works with diverse teams to identify local needs and strategize the most effective ways to achieve competing goals. Laurel is also a co-editor for Health Environments Research & Design (HERD) Journal and loves meeting with students to chat about their own career and academic goals.
